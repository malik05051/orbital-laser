**Orbital Laser 0.6.0** brings the Earthquake and its tsunami, sirens on every stretch of land, and gravity after a strike.

**The fastest way to get it:** the fork's Horizon launcher, [orbital-launcher-0.3.0](https://github.com/malik05051/horizon/releases/tag/orbital-launcher-0.3.0) or newer. It installs this release before the game starts, and every newer one after it. Settings: `orbital-mod-updates on|off`. It only ever replaces an older copy, never a newer one or one it can't read.

## New
- **The Earthquake** (`/orbital earthquake`, the wheel's fourth page): Tohoku 2011, Kobe 1995, Kanto 1923, Kumamoto 2016, Noto 2024 or Valdivia 1960, or your own (magnitude up to 50, depth up to 1000 km, how long and how hard it shakes, a tsunami from magnitude 6.5). Everything goes by the JMA Shindo scale, with the Earthquake Early Warning before the strong shaking and aftershocks after it.
- **Its tsunami comes to the coast you're near.** The real sea draws back, then the wave comes in from it (about 15 m for Tohoku), running up to where you stand. The warning comes three minutes after the quake, the first wave 3 to 8 minutes later unless you set ARRIVAL.
- **A false earthquake alert** (the wheel's FALSE ALERT, `/orbital quakefalse on`): the warning goes out but no quake comes, then it's canceled after CANCEL IN (10 s to 10 min), or at once with CANCEL beside STOP or `/orbital cancelalert`.
- **J-Alert sirens on all land**: a pole in each 160-block square near you, wherever you go, none at sea, each heard from the nearest. They sound the Earthquake's and the Tsunami's warnings, and a general alert for every other laser (`/orbital alertsirens on|off`). Volume: `/orbital sirenvolume`.
- **The Tsunami's own height, sirens and arrival**: 2 to 120 m at the coast, and a countdown of up to 30 minutes before the wave (the wheel's ARRIVAL, `/orbital tsunamiarrival`). Trees topple as falling blocks.
- **Gravity after a strike** (`/orbital gravity on|off`, on by default): what a strike leaves hanging comes down, as falling blocks near you.

## Sounds
This release has the **synthesised** sirens, Earthquake Early Warning chime, general alert and cancellation. Recordings of Japan's real alerts are someone else's, so none is in it. To use your own, put them in with `tools/sounds_quake.py --eew-recording`, `--major-recording`, `--alert-recording` and `--cancel-recording`, then build the mod yourself. NHK's alert tone and the faraway coast's sirens are silent without your recordings.
