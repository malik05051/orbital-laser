**Orbital Laser 0.7.0** brings the Tornado, a time before every strike, and the J-Alert's general alert you can hear.

**How to get it:** the fork's Horizon launcher, [orbital-launcher-0.3.0](https://github.com/malik05051/horizon/releases/tag/orbital-launcher-0.3.0) or newer, installs this release the next time you press Play.

## New
- **The Tornado** (`/orbital tornado`, the wheel's third page, next to the Hurricane):
  - **What happens:** a supercell's storm forms over the target, and one tornado comes down out of its wall cloud and crosses the land through the target for about a minute.
  - **Rating:** pick it on the wheel's RATING row or with `/orbital tornadoef ef0`–`ef5`. It follows the Enhanced Fujita scale's 3-second gusts: EF0 65–85 mph (105–137 km/h), EF1 86–110, EF2 111–135, EF3 136–165, EF4 166–200, EF5 over 200 mph (322 km/h).
  - **Stronger means more:** it's wider and its roar louder and deeper. It wrecks more as the scale's damage goes, from plants and leaves at EF0 to houses swept away and the ground scoured at EF5.
  - **Around it:** blocks are flung round it, and mobs caught in it are hurt. With KNOCKBACK on, it throws you if you're near.
- **A time before every strike** (the wheel's DELAY by the flying speed, `/orbital strikedelay`, 10 s to 30 min):
  - **What happens:** a laser fired with a delay warns first: its name on the screen, "strikes in m:ss" over the hotbar, and the general alert from the J-Alert poles round you. Then it strikes where you aimed.
  - **Exceptions:** the Earthquake's countdown has no sirens, since an earthquake can't be foreseen. The Tsunami keeps its own ARRIVAL.

## Fixed
- **The general alert is heard.** In 0.6.0 it was put after everything else in a strike, so it came too late or never. Every laser but the Earthquake and the Tsunami now sounds it from the poles round you, and the poles stand in the same places from one strike to the next.

## Sounds
As in 0.6.0, the sirens, chimes, general alert and EEW cancellation are the **synthesised** ones; no recording of the real alerts is included. Your own go in with `tools/sounds_quake.py` and a build of your own.
